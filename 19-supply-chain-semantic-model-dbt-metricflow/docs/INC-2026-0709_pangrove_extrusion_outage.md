# Incident INC-2026-0709: Pangrove Polymer extrusion line 3 outage

**What happened.** On 2026-07-09 a resin dryer on extrusion line 3 at Pangrove's Hamilton plant overheated and caught fire. Nobody was hurt. Line 3 makes the stretch film and pallet wrap that Ostrawind buys. Pangrove ran on lines 1 and 2 only until line 3 restarted on 2026-09-26.

**Root cause (Pangrove's own report).** The dryer's thermal cut-off had been on the deferred maintenance list since March 2026. Pangrove classified the event as equipment failure.

**Notification.** Pangrove's account manager mentioned the fire on a call on 2026-07-14. The written notice arrived on 2026-07-24, 11 business days after the event.

**Impact on Ostrawind.** Late and partial deliveries on stretch film, pallet wrap, cushion rolls and strapping kits from July to September. Ostrawind moved part of the volume to Cinderpath Express expedited loads to protect the Toronto Distribution Center. Toronto had stockouts on film SKUs in August and September.

**Actions.** Pangrove repaired the dryer, added the cut-off to a monthly inspection, and committed to a recovery plan with weekly allocation calls. Ostrawind procurement opened claim CLM-2026-031 for service credits and expedited freight.
