# Supply Agreement CTR-PGV-2025 between Ostrawind Supply Co. and Pangrove Polymer Ltd (excerpt)

## Schedule B, section B.1: Service levels
Pangrove Polymer Ltd ("Supplier") shall meet the following service levels, measured per calendar quarter on purchase orders whose promised delivery date falls in that quarter:
- On-time delivery (OTD): at least 92% of received purchase orders received on or before the promised delivery date.
- Fill rate: at least 98% of ordered units received on received purchase orders.
- Damage rate: at most 0.5% of units arriving damaged.

## Schedule B, section B.2: Service credits
For each service level missed in a quarter, Supplier owes a service credit of 2% of that quarter's purchase value, up to 10% of the quarter's purchase value in total. Credits are applied to the next invoice.

## Schedule B, section B.3: Expedited freight
When Supplier misses a promised date, Ostrawind may expedite remaining loads. Expedited freight above the standard rate is recoverable from Supplier for the affected quarter.

## Section 9: Force majeure
Supplier is excused for failures caused by events beyond its reasonable control (for example natural disaster, war, or a regional power grid failure) only if Supplier notifies Ostrawind in writing within 5 business days of the event. Equipment failure caused by Supplier's own maintenance practices is not force majeure.
