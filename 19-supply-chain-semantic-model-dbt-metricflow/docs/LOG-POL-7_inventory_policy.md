# LOG-POL-7: Inventory availability and stockouts

- **Available units** = on-hand units minus units allocated to open customer orders.
- A **stockout** is a month-end snapshot where available units are zero or less.
- A SKU is **below safety stock** when available units are under its safety stock units.
- Inventory snapshots are point-in-time values: never add on-hand units across snapshot dates. Use one snapshot date, or the average.
- Expedited freight needs approval from the regional supply chain manager when a stockout is expected within 10 days.
