# Temperature excursion SOP, version 3

Apply in order. Step 1: freeze-sensitive product with any reading at or below 0.0 C -> DESTROY, no exceptions. Step 2: max at most 12.0 C AND at most 30 min AND the lot has at least 60 min of stability budget left before this excursion -> RELEASE (deduct the minutes from the lot budget). Step 3: anything else -> QUARANTINE pending QA review.
