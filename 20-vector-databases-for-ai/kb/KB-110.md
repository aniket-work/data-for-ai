# High-Interest Savings account terms

## Interest

The High-Interest Savings account earns interest calculated on the daily closing balance and paid
monthly. The current posted rate is 3.00% per year; rates can change at any time without notice.

## Withdrawals

Two self-serve withdrawals per month are free; each additional withdrawal costs $5. Transfers
between your own Kestrel accounts in the app count as withdrawals.
