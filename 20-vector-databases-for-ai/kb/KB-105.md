# Mobile cheque deposit

## Limits

Mobile deposit accepts cheques up to $10,000 each and $25,000 per day per customer. Write 'Deposited
by Kestrel mobile' and the date on the back of the cheque before taking the photo.

## Keeping the paper cheque

Keep the original cheque for 30 days after the deposit, then destroy it securely. Do not deposit the
same cheque again at a branch or ABM.
