# Phishing, scam calls and protecting your account

## What Kestrel will never ask

Kestrel Bay Bank will never ask for your password, full PIN or a one-time passcode by phone, text or
email. Never share a one-time passcode with anyone, even if the caller says they are from the bank.

## If you shared information

Change your online banking password immediately, lock your cards in Card Controls (KB-102), and call
card services. Report suspicious messages using Report Fraud in the app.
