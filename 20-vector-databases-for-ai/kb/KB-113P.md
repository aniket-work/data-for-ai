# Prairies wildfire relief for mortgage and loan customers

## Eligibility

Customers whose primary residence is in Alberta, Saskatchewan or Manitoba and who were ordered to
evacuate, or whose home was damaged by a wildfire in 2026, are eligible for Prairies Wildfire
Relief.

## Relief offered

Mortgage and Personal Loan payments can be skipped for up to 6 months with no late fees, and Kestrel
waives the interest on skipped payments for the first 3 months. NSF fees are also refunded for the
affected period.

## How to apply

Apply through the Calgary, Winnipeg or Saskatoon branches, or the Financial Care team. Provide the
evacuation order or an insurance claim number.
