# Two-step verification and new device sign-in

## How it works

When you sign in to online banking from a new device, Kestrel sends a one-time passcode to the
mobile number on file. Codes expire after 10 minutes.

## Changing your phone number

If you changed your mobile number and cannot receive codes, visit a branch with photo ID or call the
digital support line; the number cannot be changed in an unverified session.
