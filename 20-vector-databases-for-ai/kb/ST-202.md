# Staff: fee reversal authority

## Limits

Front-line staff can reverse up to two NSF fees per customer per calendar year without approval.
Branch managers can reverse up to $250 in fees per customer per year. Larger reversals need regional
approval.

## Documentation

Every reversal must include a reason code in the customer profile.
