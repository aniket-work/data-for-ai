# Merchant disputes and chargebacks

## When to use

If you paid a merchant with your Kestrel credit card and the goods were never delivered, were not as
described, or you were charged twice, you can ask Kestrel to start a chargeback after first trying
to resolve it with the merchant.

## Time limits

Chargeback requests must be made within 120 days of the transaction date. Kestrel may ask for
receipts, emails or tracking information.
