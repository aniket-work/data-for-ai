# Interac e-Transfer limits and Autodeposit

## Limits

Customers can send up to $3,000 per transfer and $10,000 per rolling 7 days by Interac e-Transfer.
Limits for Small Business accounts are set in the business banking agreement.

## Autodeposit

With Autodeposit registered, incoming e-Transfers to your email address or mobile number are
deposited automatically with no security question.

## Recalling a transfer

A pending e-Transfer that the recipient has not yet accepted can be cancelled in the app; the funds
return within 30 minutes.
