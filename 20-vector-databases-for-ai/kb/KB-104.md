# Funds availability and cheque holds

## Standard availability

Cheques deposited at a branch, ABM or by mobile deposit before 8 p.m. Eastern Time on a business day
are credited the same day. Kestrel Bay Bank may place a hold on deposited cheques; the first $100 of
each deposit is always available immediately.

## Hold periods

The maximum hold period is 4 business days for cheques up to $1,500 and 5 business days for larger
cheques drawn on a Canadian financial institution. Cheques in U.S. dollars or drawn on foreign
institutions may be held up to 15 business days.

## Exceptions

Holds may be extended when an account is less than 90 days old, when a cheque is post-dated or
damaged, or when there is reason to suspect fraud. The customer is told the reason and the release
date.
