# Account closure and dormant accounts

## Closing an account

Accounts can be closed at a branch or by secure message. Any balance is paid by bank draft or
transfer. Accounts closed within 90 days of opening have a $20 early closure fee.

## Dormant accounts

An account with no customer-initiated activity for 2 years is dormant. Kestrel sends notices at 2, 5
and 9 years. After 10 years of inactivity, unclaimed balances are transferred to the Bank of Canada.
