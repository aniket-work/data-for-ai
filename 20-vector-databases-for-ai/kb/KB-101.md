# Retained debit card at an automated banking machine

## When a card is retained

An automated banking machine (ABM) may retain a debit card after three incorrect PIN entries, when
the card has expired, or when the chip cannot be read. Retained cards are never returned to the
customer; the machine destroys them during the next cash replenishment.

## What to do

Contact Kestrel Bay Bank card services on the number on the back of any Kestrel card, or use Card
Controls in the mobile app, to block the retained card. A replacement card is issued with a new card
number and mailed within 5 to 7 business days. Express replacement to a branch is available in 2
business days at no fee.

## Your access in the meantime

Mobile and online banking keep working while the replacement is on its way. Cash can be withdrawn at
any Kestrel branch with government-issued photo ID.
