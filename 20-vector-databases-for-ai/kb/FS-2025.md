# Personal banking fee schedule 2025 (superseded)

## Everyday Chequing

Monthly account fee $13.95, waived with a minimum daily balance of $3,000. Interac e-Transfer: $1.00
per transfer. Paper statement: $2.00 per month.

## Returned items

NSF-02 Non-sufficient funds item: $48.00. RPD-01 Returned pre-authorized debit (stop payment by
customer): $12.50.

## Wires and drafts

WIR-OUT International wire transfer sent: $30.00 plus intermediary bank charges. WIR-IN Incoming
wire: $15.00. DRF-01 Bank draft: $8.95.
