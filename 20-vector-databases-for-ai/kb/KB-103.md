# Unauthorized transactions and the zero liability guarantee

## Coverage

Kestrel Bay Bank reimburses cardholders for unauthorized transactions on their debit or credit card
under the Zero Liability Guarantee, provided the cardholder protected their PIN and reported the
loss or the unauthorized use promptly.

## Reporting window

Report unauthorized activity within 30 days of the statement date on which it first appears.
Disputes are opened through the mobile app (Transactions > Dispute) or by calling card services.
Provisional credit is normally applied within 10 business days while the investigation is open.

## Exclusions

The guarantee does not cover transactions the cardholder authorized, including payments to a
merchant that later failed to deliver; those follow the merchant dispute (chargeback) process in
KB-118.
