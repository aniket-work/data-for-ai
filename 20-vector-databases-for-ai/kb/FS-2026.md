# Personal banking fee schedule 2026

## Everyday Chequing

Monthly account fee $14.95, waived with a minimum daily balance of $3,000. Interac e-Transfer: free.
Paper statement: $2.50 per month.

## Returned items

NSF-02 Non-sufficient funds item: $45.00. RPD-01 Returned pre-authorized debit (stop payment by
customer): $12.50.

## Wires and drafts

WIR-OUT International wire transfer sent: $35.00 plus intermediary bank charges. WIR-IN Incoming
wire: $15.00. DRF-01 Bank draft: $9.95.
