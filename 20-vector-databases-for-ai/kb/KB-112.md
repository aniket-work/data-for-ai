# Fixed Mortgage prepayment privileges

## Annual lump sum

Each calendar year, a Fixed Mortgage borrower may prepay up to 15% of the original principal as a
lump sum without a prepayment charge. Unused privilege does not carry forward.

## Payment increase

Regular payments can be increased once per calendar year by up to 15% of the current payment amount.

## Prepayment charge

Paying more than the privilege, or paying off the mortgage before the end of the term, triggers a
prepayment charge equal to the greater of three months' interest or the interest rate differential
(IRD).
