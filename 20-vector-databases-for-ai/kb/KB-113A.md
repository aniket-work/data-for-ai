# Atlantic storm relief for mortgage customers

## Eligibility

Customers in Nova Scotia, New Brunswick, Prince Edward Island and Newfoundland and Labrador whose
home was damaged by a named hurricane or post-tropical storm in 2026 are eligible for Atlantic Storm
Relief.

## Relief offered

Mortgage payments can be deferred for up to 3 months with no late fees. Interest continues to
accrue. Kestrel also offers a repair line of credit at prime rate for up to 12 months.

## How to apply

Apply at the Halifax or Moncton branches or through the Financial Care team with an insurance claim
number.
