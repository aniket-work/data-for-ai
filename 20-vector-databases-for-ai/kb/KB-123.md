# Power of attorney on Kestrel accounts

## Registering a POA

An attorney named in a valid power of attorney document can act on the grantor's accounts once the
document has been reviewed by Kestrel's Legal Services team. Review normally takes 5 business days.

## Limits

The attorney cannot change beneficiaries or add themselves as a joint owner.
