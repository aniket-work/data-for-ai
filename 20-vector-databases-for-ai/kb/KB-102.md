# Card Controls: temporarily lock and unlock a card

## What Card Controls does

Card Controls in the Kestrel mobile app lets a cardholder place a temporary lock on a debit or
credit card. A locked card declines new purchases, ABM withdrawals and online payments. Pre-
authorized payments such as subscriptions continue.

## Locking versus reporting

Use a temporary lock if a card is misplaced and may turn up. If a card is confirmed lost or stolen,
report it instead: reporting permanently cancels the card number and orders a replacement.

## Unlocking

Unlocking takes effect immediately. A card can be locked and unlocked any number of times with no
fee.
