# Business Line of Credit annual review

## Annual review

Each Business Line of Credit is reviewed every 12 months. The business must provide year-end
financial statements within 120 days of its fiscal year end.

## Covenants

Lines above $250,000 carry a debt service coverage covenant of at least 1.25 times. A missed
covenant may lead to a reduced limit or a higher rate.
