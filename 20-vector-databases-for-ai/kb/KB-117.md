# Updating your address and moving between provinces

## Changing your address

Update your mailing and residential address in the mobile app under Profile, or at any branch.
Address changes apply to all your accounts and cards.

## Moving to another province

Accounts stay open when you move within Canada. Your home branch can be changed to the nearest
Kestrel branch. Mortgages remain on the same terms; if the property is sold, the prepayment rules in
KB-112 apply.
