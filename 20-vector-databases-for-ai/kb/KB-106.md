# Non-sufficient funds (NSF) and returned payments

## When a payment is returned

If a cheque, pre-authorized debit or bill payment is presented and the account does not have enough
available funds and no overdraft protection, the item is returned unpaid to the payee. This is
called a non-sufficient funds (NSF) item.

## Fee

The NSF fee is listed in fee schedule FS-2026 (fee code NSF-02). Kestrel Bay Bank charges at most
one NSF fee per item per business day, even if the payee presents the item again.

## Avoiding returned items

Customers can add Overdraft Protection (KB-107) or turn on Low Balance Alerts in the mobile app.
