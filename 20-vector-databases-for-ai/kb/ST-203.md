# Staff: handling a hardship call

## Script

Acknowledge the situation, do not promise an outcome, and book a Financial Care appointment within 2
business days. Record the hardship reason code in the profile.

## Regional programs

Check for active regional relief programs (for example KB-113P or KB-113A) before offering the
standard deferral in KB-113.
