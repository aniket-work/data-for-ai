# Safety deposit boxes

## Availability

Safety deposit boxes are offered at the Toronto King West, Ottawa Glebe and Montreal Plateau
branches only. Annual rent starts at $65 for a small box.

## Access

Box holders can access the box during branch hours with photo ID. Kestrel does not insure the
contents.
