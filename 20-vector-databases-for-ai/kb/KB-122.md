# Joint accounts and survivorship

## Ownership

A joint account is owned by two or more people. Each owner can make deposits and withdrawals unless
the account requires all signatures.

## When an owner dies

For joint accounts with right of survivorship, the funds pass to the surviving owner. The bank needs
a death certificate to update the account. Estates are handled by the Estate Services team.
