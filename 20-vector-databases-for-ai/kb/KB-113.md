# Mortgage payment deferral and financial hardship

## Who qualifies

Borrowers facing financial hardship, such as job loss, serious illness, or the death of a co-
borrower, can ask the Financial Care team for help. Options include a payment deferral of up to 4
months, a temporary interest-only period, or extending the amortization.

## What a deferral means

During a deferral, interest continues to accrue and is added to the mortgage balance. A deferral is
not payment forgiveness.

## How to ask

Contact the Financial Care team before missing a payment. Kestrel Bay Bank reviews each request
individually and may ask for proof of income change.
