# Overdraft Protection

## How it works

Overdraft Protection covers payments up to an approved limit when the chequing balance is not
enough. Interest of 21% per year applies to the overdrawn balance, calculated daily.

## Fees

The monthly Overdraft Protection fee is $5 in any month the account is overdrawn and $0 otherwise.
There is no NSF fee for items covered by Overdraft Protection.
