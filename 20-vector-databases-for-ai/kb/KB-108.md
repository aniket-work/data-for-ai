# Sending an international wire transfer

## What you need

To send money to a bank account outside Canada you need the recipient's full name and address, the
receiving bank's SWIFT/BIC code, and the account number or IBAN. Some countries also require a
purpose-of-payment code.

## Limits and timing

Wires up to $10,000 per day can be sent in online banking. Larger wires must be requested at a
branch using form KB-114. Most wires arrive within 1 to 3 business days; intermediary banks may
deduct their own charges.

## Fees and exchange

The outgoing wire fee is fee code WIR-OUT in fee schedule FS-2026. Foreign exchange is converted at
Kestrel's posted rate at the time the wire is sent.
