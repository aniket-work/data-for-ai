# Kestrel Bay Bank personal account agreement

## 1. About this agreement

This agreement applies to every personal deposit account you hold with Kestrel Bay Bank, including
Everyday Chequing and High-Interest Savings, together with the account disclosures, the fee schedule
and any program terms we give you. In this agreement 'you' means each account holder and 'we' means
Kestrel Bay Bank. If this agreement conflicts with a program term, the program term applies to that
program only. Please read it together with the current personal banking fee schedule, which forms
part of it, and keep a copy for your records.

## 2. Opening and identification

To open an account we must verify your identity using government-issued photo identification or
other methods allowed by law. We may refuse to open an account, or may restrict it, if we cannot
verify your identity, if the account would be used for an unlawful purpose, or if you have given us
false information. You must tell us promptly if your name, address, phone number, email address,
citizenship or tax residency changes, so that our records stay accurate.

## 3. Deposits

You can make deposits at a branch, at an automated banking machine, by mobile deposit, or by
electronic transfer. Every deposit is subject to verification and to our funds availability rules.
If an item you deposit is later returned unpaid, for any reason, we will debit its amount from your
account, together with any fee shown in the fee schedule, even if you have already used the funds.

## 4. Withdrawals and payments

You may withdraw available funds by any channel we offer, subject to daily limits. We may refuse a
withdrawal or payment if the account does not have enough available funds, if the instruction is
incomplete or unclear, or if we suspect fraud or unauthorized use. We are not required to make a
payment that would overdraw the account unless you have overdraft protection.

## 5. Statements and errors

We provide a statement each month, or a passbook update, for each account. You must review every
statement and tell us about any error, omission or unauthorized entry within 60 days of the
statement date. After those 60 days, the statement is considered correct and final, except for
amounts improperly credited to you, which we may always reverse.

## 6. Interest

Interest on interest-bearing accounts is calculated daily on the closing balance and paid monthly at
the rate we post. We can change posted rates at any time. Accounts with a negative balance do not
earn interest, and interest on overdrawn balances is charged as set out in the overdraft protection
terms.

## 7. Changes to fees and to this agreement

We may change this agreement or any fee. We will give you at least 30 days' written notice before a
new fee or a fee increase takes effect, by statement message, by notice in our branches and online,
or by email. If you do not accept a change, you may close the account without paying any closure fee
within 30 days after the change takes effect.

## 8. Right of set-off

If you owe us money that is due and unpaid on any loan, line of credit, mortgage or credit card, we
may use money in any of your deposit accounts, including joint accounts, to pay that debt, without
prior notice to you. This is called the right of set-off. We will tell you after we have done so.

## 9. Dormant and unclaimed balances

If there is no customer-initiated activity on an account for two years, the account becomes dormant,
and dormant account notices are sent as described in our dormant account policy. Balances that
remain unclaimed for ten years are transferred to the Bank of Canada as required by law.

## 10. Closing your account

You can close your account at any time. We can close your account by giving you 30 days' notice. We
may close it immediately and without prior notice if you have been abusive to our staff, if we
suspect fraud, or if keeping it open would break the law or expose us to legal or reputational risk.

## 11. Complaints

If you have a complaint, contact your branch or the customer care centre first. If you are not
satisfied, escalate it to the Kestrel Bay Bank Office of the Ombudsman. If your complaint is not
resolved within 56 days, or you are not satisfied with our final answer, you may take it to the
independent external complaints body approved for our bank, at no cost to you.

## 12. Privacy

We collect, use and disclose your personal information as described in the Kestrel Bay Bank privacy
code. We do not sell your personal information. You can withdraw consent for marketing at any time,
but not for uses required to operate your account or required by law.

## 13. Our liability

We are responsible for direct losses caused by our gross negligence or wilful misconduct. We are not
liable for indirect losses, or for losses caused by events beyond our reasonable control, such as
power failures, natural disasters or the failure of a payment network.

## 14. Electronic communications

You agree that we may send you notices, statements and disclosures electronically, through online
banking or to the email address you gave us. Electronic notices have the same effect as paper
notices. You can ask for paper copies, and paper statement fees in the fee schedule may apply.

## 15. Governing law

This agreement is governed by the laws of the province or territory in Canada where your home branch
is located, and by the federal laws of Canada that apply there. If you move, your home branch, and
so the governing law, changes only when we update your home branch at your request.
