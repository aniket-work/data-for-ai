# Branch wire request form KB-114

## Purpose

Form KB-114 is used for wire transfers above the online daily limit of $10,000, and for wires to
countries that require additional documentation.

## Completing the form

Bring photo ID and the recipient details. A branch officer verifies the request by calling the
customer back on the phone number on file before the wire is released.
