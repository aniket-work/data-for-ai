# Personal Loan: missed payments and collections

## Late payments

A Personal Loan payment not received within 10 days of the due date is late. A $25 late fee applies
and the missed payment may be reported to the credit bureaus after 30 days.

## Getting help

Customers who expect to miss a payment should contact the Financial Care team. Options include
moving the payment date or a short-term reduced payment plan.
