# 1-Year GIC: early redemption and renewal

## Product

The 1-Year GIC (product code GIC-1Y) pays a fixed 4.25% per year at maturity. Minimum investment
$500.

## Early redemption

The 1-Year GIC is non-redeemable. In cases of death or documented financial hardship, the bank may
allow early redemption with interest reduced to 0.50% per year for the time held.

## Renewal

At maturity the GIC renews automatically for another one-year term at the posted rate unless the
customer gives instructions within 10 days after maturity.
