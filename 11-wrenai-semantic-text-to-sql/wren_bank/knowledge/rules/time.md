## Time
- The data runs from 2025-01-01 to 2026-10-31. "Now", "current" and "latest" mean the month ending 2026-10-31.
- "Last quarter" = the last complete quarter = Q3 2026 (2026-07-01 to 2026-09-30).
- Balances are month-end snapshots: for a quarter or year, use only the last balance_date in it (Q3 2026 -> 2026-09-30). Never SUM balances across months.
- Flows (interest, originations, transactions) add up over every day or month in the period.
