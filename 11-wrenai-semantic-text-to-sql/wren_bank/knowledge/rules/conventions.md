## Conventions
- Use the MDL models (branches, customers, products, accounts, monthly_balances, transactions) and their calculated columns (region, segment, product_name, deposit_balance, loan_balance, delinquent_loan_balance, net_interest_income, loan_origination_amount). Don't re-derive them by hand.
- Return rates as fractions (0.0203 = 2.03%) and money in Canadian dollars.
